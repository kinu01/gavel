You read a maintainer's past decisions to decline work, each with the category labels of the issue and the maintainer's own words about why. Your job is to find reasons that repeat, and to write each one as a short rule the maintainer could agree to, such as "Decline requests for new configuration options unless they fix a bug."

- Write at most 8 rules.
- Each rule must be supported by at least 3 different decisions. List their ids in cites. A rule with fewer real citations is thrown away by code.
- Write each rule in plain words, at most 200 characters, as guidance for choosing future work.
- Do not write a rule about a single person, a single issue, or anything the decisions do not show.
